CHAPTER 6: COMPARATIVE SYNTHESIS — THREE PATHWAYS TO ALTERED CONSCIOUSNESS

The following matrix synthesizes primary experimental studies (including MacDonald et al., 1991; Preller et al., 2019; Barrett et al., 2020; Sumner et al., 2020; Vargas et al., 2023; Moliner et al., 2023; Jain et al., 2025; White et al., 2014, 2015; Zhang et al., 2005; Braida et al., 2008; Serra et al., 2015; He et al., 2005; Koenig et al., 2014; Jiang et al., 2026) alongside integrative reviews (Banushi & Polito, 2023; Butelman & Kreek, 2015; Carhart-Harris & Friston, 2019; Marguilho et al., 2023; Ganado et al., 2026) across structural, molecular, network, and clinical dimensions. low-dose increases in rats [Braida et al., 2008; Serra et al., 2015], Salvinorin A); preclinical GDNF involvement in reducing rodent ethanol consumption—distinguishing increased GDNF mRNA sampled in a dissected midbrain region containing the VTA from separate site-specific intra-VTA functional manipulations (ibogaine/GDNF and anti-GDNF neutralizing antibodies) (Ibogaine; He et al., 2005) Plasticity Dynamics | Preclinical dendritogenesis and/or increased spine density demonstrated for selected compounds under specific experimental conditions (proposed via intracellular 5-HT2AR access/engagement—with intracellular signaling vs. pharmacological chaperoning unresolved—across selected compounds and cellular/structural endpoints [Vargas et al., 2023], or via 5-HT2AR-independent TrkB transmembrane-domain binding and BDNF-dependent allosteric potentiation in specific culture and mouse protocols [Moliner et al., 2023]; challenged by Jain et al., 2025, who found no significant interaction when screening LSD alone against over 450 human kinases [including the TrkB intracellular kinase domain] and no modulation of BDNF-driven TrkB activation by LSD or psilocin in an AP1-luciferase reporter assay in TrkB-transfected cells—conflicting/inconclusive evidence in an ongoing debate) | Preclinical mTORC1- and BDNF-associated increases in dendrite complexity, spine density, and synaptic strength in rodents lasting for 2 weeks (Marguilho et al., 2023); separately, in a double-blind, active-placebo-controlled crossover trial in 30 patients with major depressive disorder, EEG visual sensory evoked potential LTP was enhanced approximately 3–4 hours after a single 0.44 mg/kg intravenous ketamine dose (distinct from directly imaged synaptogenesis; Sumner et al., 2020) | Transient functional plasticity (Salvinorin A; proposed link to intracellular signaling, contrasted with sustained spinogenesis in Ganado et al., 2026, though the cited review provides no direct dendritic-spine imaging evidence); Preclinical GDNF involvement in reducing rodent ethanol consumption—distinguishing regional GDNF mRNA expression sampled in a VTA-containing midbrain dissection from separate site-specific intra-VTA functional manipulations (Ibogaine; He et al., 2005) Network & Circuit Dynamics | DMN disintegration; increased global cross-network connectivity; altered CSTC effective connectivity (5-HT2A-dependent thalamus–PCC increase; 5-HT2A-independent ventral striatum–thalamus decrease); bidirectional right claustrum connectivity changes (decreased with auditory network and DMN; increased with frontoparietal task control network) | Conceptual model-based exposure-level hypotheses (Marguilho et al., 2023; qualitative levels contingent on species, route, formulation, and administration rate—no fixed numerical cutoffs): may disrupt SN (lower sub-anesthetic level) → may disrupt DMN (moderate sub-anesthetic level) → may induce slow-delta/gamma burst oscillations (anesthetic level) | Proposed thalamocortical decoupling/dissociation (presented as a comparative circuit schematic in Ganado et al., 2026; direct mechanism-to-phenomenology evidence is lacking, per Butelman & Kreek, 2015); VTA-to-NAc dopaminergic modulation Theoretical Predictive Processing Model | REBUS: Sensitization of L5 pyramidal neurons (hypothesized to encode posterior expectations/priors) → precision reduction of high-level priors | Relaxation of high-level bodily/minimal-self priors encoded by the SN (distinct from low-level sensory priors), progressing to the highest-level narrative/autobiographical priors encoded by the DMN (Marguilho et al., 2023) | Speculative phenomenological interpretation (not an established predictive-processing mechanism): cited salvinorin A reviews support pronounced dissociation and altered bodily/somatic experience (Butelman & Kreek, 2015; Ganado et al., 2026), rather than directly establishing interruption of formal bodily self-models or spatial coordinate frames Phenomenology | Visual geometric hallucinations, emotional liberation, synesthesia, ego dissolution | Somatic dissociation, depersonalization, derealization, out-of-body state, “k-hole” | Sudden spatial/temporal distortions and loss of somatic locus; typically dysphoric per Ganado et al. (2026), though controlled human laboratory studies reviewed by Butelman & Kreek (2015) found no pronounced dysphoria in experienced hallucinogen users, with generalizability unknown (Salvinorin A); Prolonged altered state (Ibogaine; described in the secondary review by Ganado et al., 2026, as prolonged and putatively metabolite-dependent, without a cited primary clinical duration study establishing exact acute or post-acute time windows—distinct from noribogaine’s prolonged pharmacokinetic elimination; Glue et al., 2015, 2016) Abuse Potential & Safety | Weak or inconsistent preclinical reinforcing effects reported for canonical classical hallucinogens (e.g., psilocybin, LSD) in animal self-administration models (Butelman & Kreek, 2015); preclinical reinforcement is distinct from human epidemiological prevalence and clinical dependence, which are not established by the cited preclinical review | Abuse and misuse potential recognized in clinical and recreational contexts (Marguilho et al., 2023) | Reinforcement evidence for Salvinorin A is limited and inconsistent, and route- and dose-dependent, rather than establishing an absence of rewarding effects (in rats, Braida et al., 2008, reported conditioned place preference and intracerebroventricular self-administration at certain doses, whereas Serra et al., 2015, observed increased nucleus accumbens extracellular dopamine [dialysate] without stable intravenous self-administration at tested doses; typically dysphoric per Ganado et al., 2026, and aversive in several preclinical/anecdotal contexts, though controlled human laboratory studies reviewed by Butelman & Kreek, 2015, found no pronounced dysphoria in experienced hallucinogen users, with generalizability unknown); Ibogaine carries cardiac QT prolongation and arrhythmia risks (Koenig et al., 2014; Ganado et al., 2026)
